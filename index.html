The community

A neighborhood built around the golf course.

NewKairo is a fully integrated destination in East Cairo, between the Cairo–Suez and Cairo–Ismailia roads next to New Heliopolis. An 18-hole golf course sits at its heart, surrounded by homes delivered fully finished, two downtowns, schools, a sports club and hotels. Developed by G Developments.

krypton

Serviced residences for future living.

Krypton is a new phase of 1, 2 and 3 bedroom serviced apartments. The 1 and 2 bedrooms sit above the promenade and commercial area. The 3 bedrooms overlook the golf course. Every unit is delivered fully finished with ACs, and all hotel services are run by G Developments.

The rare one
Golf course views from your balcony.
Serviced apartments overlooking a golf course are almost impossible to find in Cairo. The Krypton 3 bedrooms give you exactly that.
